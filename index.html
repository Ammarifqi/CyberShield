    If this happened for real

    1. If you suspect the device itself is actively compromised, stop using it for sensitive accounts and disconnect it from the network.
    2. From a different trusted device, change affected passwords, sign out unknown sessions and turn on multi-factor authentication.
    3. Contact the service or your organisation's IT support using official details. Contact your bank promptly if money may be involved.

    These are general suggestions, not a diagnosis. The prototype has not inspected your device.

    Key takeaway Verify unusual activity through a trusted device and official channels.
